## 📌 Executive Summary & Project Objectives

**MetroFlow** is an AI-powered public transit intelligence platform designed
for predictive crowd management, passenger demand forecasting, and schedule
optimization.

The system analyzes historical public transportation data to:

- Predict passenger demand
- Forecast station crowd levels
- Identify peak hours
- Detect overcrowding risks
- Support train scheduling decisions
- Provide data-driven transportation insights

---

## 🎯 Core Objectives

### 1. Predictive Crowd Management

Forecast station-level passenger demand and identify potential
overcrowding before it occurs.

### 2. Passenger Demand Forecasting

Use machine learning models to predict future passenger traffic based on:

- Date
- Hour
- Station
- Historical ridership
- Peak-hour patterns

### 3. Peak Hour Detection

Identify high-demand periods and busiest metro stations.

### 4. Train Schedule Optimization

Use predicted passenger demand to support improved train scheduling.

### 5. Real-Time Monitoring

Provide a backend API for monitoring passenger demand and crowd conditions.
