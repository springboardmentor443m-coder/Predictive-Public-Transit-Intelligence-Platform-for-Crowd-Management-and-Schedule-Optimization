# BMRCL Ridership Analysis – Initial Findings

## Dataset Overview

- Dataset contains 92,288 records.
- Dataset contains 4 columns:
  - Date
  - Hour
  - Station
  - Ridership
- There are 83 unique stations.
- There are 48 unique dates.
- Hour values range from 0 to 23.
- No missing values were found.
- No duplicate rows were found.

## Hourly Ridership Findings

The highest average ridership occurs during the evening period.

Top 5 peak hours:

1. Hour 18 – approximately 866 passengers per record
2. Hour 9 – approximately 808 passengers per record
3. Hour 17 – approximately 746 passengers per record
4. Hour 19 – approximately 713 passengers per record
5. Hour 8 – approximately 685 passengers per record

The lowest average ridership occurs during the early morning hours.

## Station-wise Findings

The stations with the highest total ridership include:

1. Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station, Majestic
2. Benniganahalli
3. Indiranagar
4. Mahatma Gandhi Road
5. Krishnarajapura

The analysis also identified stations with comparatively lower total ridership.

## Initial Project Insight

The hourly and station-wise patterns can be used as a starting point for:

- Identifying peak-hour congestion
- Crowd monitoring
- Passenger demand forecasting
- Station-wise crowd analysis
- Future train-frequency recommendations
